Dynamic Mini Map

Your world, reimagined. Navigate with a live, GTA-inspired mini-map right on your wrist. Whether you're walking, running, or cycling, stay oriented with style.

Customizable Markers

Mark your territory. Drop pins on your iPhone and sync them instantly to your Apple Watch. Track your favorite spots, hidden gems, and daily destinations.

Augmented Reality (AR) Mode

See beyond the map. Switch to AR mode on your iPhone to visualize markers in the real world. Navigation has never been this immersive.

Fitness and Health Integration

Level up your fitness. Track your walks with a gaming-inspired health bar. Monitor your heart rate and activity rings while you explore.
